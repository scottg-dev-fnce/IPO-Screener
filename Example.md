# Example Memo

This page shows a complete sample memo produced by the IPO Screener, demonstrating the full output of the analysis pipeline including the cover page, scoring framework, red flag analysis, valuation methodology, and all 19 analytical sections.

[See the full sample memo: Sample Memo PDF](examples/Sample_Memo.pdf)

---

## What This Memo Demonstrates

- **Cover page** with composite score gauge and per-dimension score breakdown
- **Executive summary** with deal committee recommendation and supporting rationale
- **Deal committee recommendation** — one of four tiers (UNDERWRITE / CONDITIONAL LIGHT / CONDITIONAL HEAVY / PASS)
- **19 detailed analytical sections** covering every aspect of the deal from business overview to ESG disclosure
- **Red flags with severity ratings** — CRITICAL, HIGH, MEDIUM, and LOW flags mapped to affected scoring dimensions
- **Sector-specific valuation framework** — metric selection routed by SIC code and business model (EV/EBITDA, EV/Revenue, P/B, EV/EBITDAX, SOTP, and others)
- **Comparable company analysis** with 6-criterion institutional selection filter (revenue scale, geography, business model, profitability profile, listing exchange, recency)
- **Lockup analysis with timeline** — structure type, release schedule, early-release triggers, and investor-friendliness assessment
- **ESG disclosure scoring** — Bloomberg-methodology composite across Environmental, Social, and Governance pillars
